Money Trivia

Can you guess the right answers before we start the unit?

1. What are U.S. paper bills mostly made of?

Correct! U.S. currency is actually a blend of 75% cotton and 25% linen. That's why it survives going through the washing machine!

2. Who prints more money annually?

Correct! Parker Brothers prints about $30 billion in Monopoly money every year, which is way more than the US Mint prints in real money.

3. Where was paper money first invented?

Correct! The first paper money was created in China during the Tang Dynasty (7th century) because heavy copper coins were too difficult for merchants to carry.
